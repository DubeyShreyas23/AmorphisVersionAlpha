You can integrate our parts onto any spacecraft going anywhere, without filing for a US State Department export licence (typically 12–18 months and not always granted). Tier-2 satellite manufacturers — most of the global market — simply cannot afford the ITAR overhead. Our parts unlock that market.
</details>
